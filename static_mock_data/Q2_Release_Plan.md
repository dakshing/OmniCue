# Q2 2026 Engineering Roadmap and Release Plan

## Executive Summary
This document outlines the critical dates, milestones, and deliverables for our upcoming Q2 product launch. All engineering pods must align their sprint goals with these milestones to ensure a smooth and on-time delivery of the v4.2 release.

## Key Milestones
- **Architecture Review Complete**: April 10, 2026
- **Feature Complete (Alpha)**: May 20, 2026
- **Internal Dogfooding Begins**: May 25, 2026
- **QA Regression Testing**: June 1 - June 10, 2026
- **Code Freeze is officially scheduled for June 15, 2026. No further main-branch pushes are permitted.**
- **Staging Environment Lock**: June 18, 2026
- **Production Deployment (GA)**: June 22, 2026

## Pod Objectives
### Frontend Pod
- Implement the new OmniCue floating card overlay UI.
- Ensure cross-browser compatibility (Chrome, Edge, Safari).
- Reduce main bundle size by 15%.

### Backend Pod
- Finalize the WebSocket proxy infrastructure.
- Optimize the Elasticsearch MCP tool latency.
- Complete the migration to the new PostgreSQL schema.

### Data & AI Pod
- Train and deploy the updated intent recognition model.
- Tune ELSER embeddings for better technical jargon matching.

## Risk Management
Any delays in the QA Regression Testing phase will directly impact the production deployment date. Critical path blockers must be escalated to the Director of Engineering immediately.
