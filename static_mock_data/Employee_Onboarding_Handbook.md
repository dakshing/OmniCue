# Employee Onboarding: IT Setup & Hardware Policy

## Welcome to the Team!
We are thrilled to have you join us. This document outlines the IT provisioning process and hardware policies to get you set up for success on your first day.

## Identity and Access
Upon your first day, IT will provision your **Okta SSO (Single Sign-On)** credentials. This is your master key to access all internal applications, including:
- Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Drive)
- Slack
- Confluence & Jira
- GitHub Enterprise
- Workday

Please set up your Okta Verify mobile application immediately upon receiving your welcome email.

## Hardware Budget and Selection
Every full-time employee receives a hardware budget to customize their workstation.
- **Standard Budget**: $2,500 USD
- **Approved Devices**: 
  - Apple MacBook Pro (14" or 16", M2/M3 chips)
  - Dell XPS 15 (Windows/Linux)
- **Peripherals**: You may use your budget to purchase monitors, keyboards, mice, and ergonomic chairs.

### Expense Process
Please purchase your approved equipment and submit your expense requests via the internal Workday portal within your first 30 days. Receipts are required for all purchases over $25.

## Security Reminders
- Never write down your passwords. Use the company-provided 1Password family account.
- Lock your screen whenever you step away from your device (`Cmd + Ctrl + Q` on Mac).
- Report any suspicious emails or phishing attempts to `#security-alerts` in Slack.
