# Customer Support Service Level Agreements (SLA)

## Overview
We offer three distinct tiers of support for our B2B clients to accommodate varying needs for availability, response times, and dedicated assistance.

## Support Tiers

### 1. Standard Tier
- **Target Audience**: Startups and small businesses.
- **Uptime Guarantee**: 99.9% (Approx. 43 minutes of downtime allowed per month).
- **Response Time**: 24-hour initial response time for non-critical tickets.
- **Support Channels**: Email and community forum support only.
- **Operating Hours**: Standard business hours (9 AM - 5 PM EST, Monday - Friday).

### 2. Premium Tier
- **Target Audience**: Mid-market companies with mission-critical operations.
- **Uptime Guarantee**: 99.95% (Approx. 21 minutes of downtime allowed per month).
- **Response Time**: 4-hour initial response time for high-priority tickets.
- **Support Channels**: Email, in-app chat, and 24/7 emergency phone support.
- **Operating Hours**: 24/7 for critical incidents, standard hours for general inquiries.

### 3. Enterprise Tier
- **Target Audience**: Fortune 500 companies and heavily regulated industries.
- **Uptime Guarantee**: 99.99% (Approx. 4 minutes of downtime allowed per month).
- **Response Time**: 1-hour critical response time (Severity 1 issues).
- **Support Channels**: All channels + dedicated Slack/Teams connect channel.
- **Additional Benefits**: 
  - Dedicated Technical Account Manager (TAM).
  - Prioritized bug fixes and feature requests in the engineering backlog.
  - Quarterly business and architecture reviews.

## SLA Penalties
If uptime drops below the guaranteed thresholds, customers are eligible for service credits. See the billing portal for instructions on claiming SLA credits.
