# Q3 Marketing Campaign: "OmniCue Everywhere"

## Campaign Overview
The "OmniCue Everywhere" campaign is designed to drive adoption of our new real-time meeting companion tool among mid-market enterprise teams. The core message focuses on time saved, reduced context switching, and the elimination of manual note-taking.

## Target Audience
- Technical Program Managers (TPMs)
- Engineering Managers
- Product Managers

## Key Value Propositions (KVPs)
1. **Zero-Friction Context**: Get the data you need without ever tabbing away from your Zoom or Google Meet window.
2. **Secure by Design**: Processing happens locally or within secure SOC2 compliant boundaries. Your meetings are never used to train external LLMs.
3. **ROI**: Save an estimated 4.5 hours per employee per week on meeting follow-ups and documentation hunting. ($4.73/user/mo ROI based on average salaries).

## Channels and Budget
- **LinkedIn Sponsored Content**: $50,000 budget. Focus on short, 15-second video demonstrations of the floating UI card appearing during a technical discussion.
- **Tech Podcasts**: $30,000 budget. Sponsorship reads on "Software Engineering Daily" and "Syntax".
- **Webinars**: Two live webinars demonstrating the Elasticsearch and GCP Agent Builder integration for technical decision-makers.

## Success Metrics
- 5,000 new top-of-funnel leads (MQLs).
- 500 scheduled sales demos.
- $1.2M in pipeline generation.
